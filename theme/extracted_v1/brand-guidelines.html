Unilistics brand guidelines

Version 1.0 / working draft / 2026-10-24

Purpose

From "I want to study abroad" to "I have started my education abroad."

Guide students from study-abroad intent to starting their education abroad, continuously explaining the next useful action.

Positioning

A calm, practical planning companion for students exploring destinations, comparing study options, preparing evidence and tracking their journey. Explainability is the promise: show what is known, what remains unknown and where published facts came from.

Personality

  • Clear: specific facts, readable hierarchy and direct actions.
  • Supportive: help without pressure, urgency or mandatory discovery gates.
  • Trustworthy: preserve uncertainty and attribute evidence.
  • Composed: quiet surfaces, restrained depth and purposeful motion.

Voice and writing

  • Use plain language, sentence case and active verbs.
  • Name the action: Save changes, View programme, Compare options.
  • Explain what happened and how to recover; retain the student's draft.
  • Never turn a criteria checklist into admission odds or promise acceptance, funding or visa validity.
  • No emoji, decorative claims, fabricated totals or unsupported best-value labels.

Identity and logo

Use the supplied geometry and its original proportions. The app wordmark is lowercase in bundled DM Sans Bold. Horizontal exports reproduce the existing 36px icon, 20px wordmark and 10px gap at five times scale. Use original greens on light surfaces, approved reversed colours on dark surfaces and supplied monochrome variants where required. Never redraw, stretch, crop, rotate, add effects or recolour the mark arbitrarily.

Palette

Forest leads primary actions in light appearance; citron highlights selected emphasis. Dark appearance uses the dedicated light action colour for contrast. Logo greens are distinct from action colours. Pair every accent with its designated content colour. Keep original country-flag colours independent of brand tokens.

Typography

DM Sans is the single product family. Use regular 400 for reading, medium 500 for supporting emphasis, semibold 600 for headings and controls, bold 700 for the wordmark or established strong emphasis. Let long names wrap. Avoid all-caps labels, forced ellipses and unnecessary typeface changes. The downloadable font includes its SIL Open Font License.

Layout and components

Use semantic surfaces, generous reading space and existing shared controls. Current tokens: phone gutter 1rem, desktop gutter 2.5rem, panel padding 1.5rem, section space 2rem, control radius 1rem and card radius 1.5rem. Existing touch token is 3rem; never fall below 44px. Keep navigation and primary actions clear of native safe areas. Reuse one component system across web, tablet and phone.

Motion

Context changes fade the changing region; drill navigation expresses hierarchy; continuity connects a card's title and logo to its detail. Current token durations are 180ms, 320ms and 360ms respectively. Keep structure and controls stable, preserve Back position and respect reduced motion. Avoid stretching text or animating whole reading surfaces.

Illustrations

Use the approved editorial landmark vignettes: forest, sage and citron, softened linework and transparent sky. Preserve organically tapered ground and water with transparent margins on every side. Show the entire illustration without cropping. These are decorative illustrations, never verified location photographs. Missing country art uses a neutral globe, never another country's landmark. Do not redistribute the third-party flag subset as a brand icon library.

Source and status

This kit records the existing owner-directed product identity; it does not introduce a rebrand. Source identity and geometry remain in brand.ts; all colour, type, geometry and motion tokens remain in theme.css. The current direction remains subject to owner review. Refresh exported assets when those sources change.

Logo clear space and minimum use

For new artwork, reserve at least half the mark height on each side. Existing in-app component spacing remains unchanged. Use the compact icon at 24px or standard icon at 36px; wordmark-only typography starts at 16px. Print clear space and minimum sizes need a final production proof. The illustrated misuse board is reference-only and must never be treated as a logo library.

Core palette contrast

  • Forest action: #245b42; RGB 36, 91, 66; CMYK 60, 0, 27, 64. White text 7.92:1 (AAA); black text 2.65:1 (Fail).
  • Citron highlight: #d7ee83; RGB 215, 238, 131; CMYK 10, 0, 45, 7. White text 1.28:1 (Fail); black text 16.45:1 (AAA).
  • Light ink: #19261f; RGB 25, 38, 31; CMYK 34, 0, 18, 85. White text 15.69:1 (AAA); black text 1.34:1 (Fail).
  • Light canvas: #f6f8f5; RGB 246, 248, 245; CMYK 1, 0, 1, 3. White text 1.07:1 (Fail); black text 19.66:1 (AAA).
  • Sage illustration: #b4d0b6; RGB 180, 208, 182; CMYK 13, 0, 12, 18. White text 1.66:1 (Fail); black text 12.64:1 (AAA).

Contrast uses relative luminance against reference white and black. AA normal text starts at 4.5:1; AAA at 7:1; 3:1 applies only to large text. Other foreground/background combinations need their own check. CMYK is a direct RGB conversion, not an ICC-managed print specification or Pantone match.

We are / we are not

  • Clear: short sentences and named actions, not jargon or vague promises.
  • Supportive: practical next steps, not pressure or artificial urgency.
  • Trustworthy: sourced facts and explicit unknowns, not invented scores or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Composed: restrained colour and useful motion, not visual noise or constant animation.

Applications

Business card, letterhead and social post previews show the same logo, font and semantic palette. They are examples for review, not approved print-ready or campaign files. No private identity, address, phone number or legal details have been invented. New photography should be truthful, permission-cleared and relevant; approved landmark artwork remains clearly illustrated rather than photographic.
