## 2. Country-by-Country Workflow Extraction

### 2.1 Germany

#### A. Institutional Framework & Entry Routes
- **Institutional Types:** Research Universities (*Universitäten*), Universities of Applied Sciences (*Fachhochschulen* / UAS), and Private Institutions. Public universities and UAS charge zero tuition fees for standard degree programs, collecting only a statutory Semester Contribution (*Semesterbeitrag*) of approximately €150–€500 per semester (including regional transit ticket).
- **Secondary School Equivalency (12 vs. 13 Years):**  
  Pakistani Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC / FSC / ICS) represents 12 years of formal education. German secondary school leaving credential (*Abitur*) spans 13 years. Consequently, HSSC holders do **not** qualify for direct entry (*Direktzugang*) to German Bachelor's programs.
- **Bridging Pathways for Bachelor's:**
  - *Pathway 1 (Local University Completion):* Complete 2 semesters (minimum 1 academic year) at an HEC-recognized Pakistani university OR complete an HEC-recognized 2-year Associate Degree Program (ADP) / BSc. Grants subject-specific direct Bachelor's eligibility.
  - *Pathway 2 (Studienkolleg + FSP):* Enroll in a 1-year preparatory college (*Studienkolleg*) in Germany (T-Kurs for tech/engineering, M-Kurs for medicine/biology, W-Kurs for economics, G/S-Kurs for humanities). Requires German language proficiency (CEFR B1/B2) and passing the entrance exam (*Aufnahmetest*). Concludes with the *Feststellungsprüfung* (FSP).
  - *Cambridge A-Levels / IB:* Directly qualify for *Direktzugang* if required subject combinations (math, sciences, languages) are fulfilled.
- **Master's Degree Entry:**  
  Requires an HEC-recognized 4-year Bachelor's degree (16 years of education, 120–130 credit hours / equivalent to 180–240 ECTS credits). The issuing institution must hold **H+ status** on the official German *Anabin* database. Strict subject-to-subject module matching applies.

#### B. Application Portals & Mechanics
- **Uni-Assist vs. Direct Portals:**  
  Applications are submitted either via Uni-Assist (standard processing fee: €75 for first application, €30 per additional choice) or directly via university institutional portals. Some universities require a Preliminary Review Documentation (*Vorprüfungsdokumentation* / VPD) through Uni-Assist prior to direct portal upload.
- **Language Requirements:**
  - English-taught Master's: IELTS Academic (minimum 6.5 overall, no sub-band below 6.0) or TOEFL iBT (85–90+).
  - German-taught programs: CEFR B2 to C1 (proven via TestDaF, DSH-2, or Goethe-Zertifikat C1).

#### C. Pakistani Document Attestation & Legalization Pipeline
1. **Secondary (Matric & Intermediate):** BISE Board verification in sealed envelope -> IBCC attestation on original reverse -> MOFA authentication.
2. **Higher Education (Bachelor's & Transcripts):** University Controller verification -> HEC online ticket + physical QR stamp -> MOFA authentication.
3. **Consular Legalization:** Copies certified at German Mission appointments or German Embassy-designated partner attestation slots.

#### D. Financial Solvency & Blocked Account (*Sperrkonto*)
- Mandatory statutory subsistence proof deposited into a regulated German blocked account provider (e.g., Expatrio, Coracle, Fintiba).
- Historical/Guide Benchmark: €11,208 to €11,904 per year (€934 to €992/month). *(Flagged for official review).*

#### E. Consular Visa Execution
- **Jurisdiction:** German Embassy Islamabad (residents of Punjab, KPK, Islamabad, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan) vs. German Consulate General Karachi (residents of Sindh and Balochistan).
- **Process:** Waitlist registration on the official German Federal Foreign Office appointment system, dossier assembly, in-person biometric interview, proof of health insurance (statutory public like TK/Barmer/AOK or incoming travel insurance), and subsequent visa stamping.

### 2.2 France

#### A. Institutional Framework & Entry Routes
- **Institutional Types:** Public Universities (*Universités Publiques*), *Grandes Écoles*, and Private Business/Specialized Schools.
- **Tuition Framework:**  
  Under the *Bienvenue en France* policy, public non-EU tuition is statutory at €2,900/year for *Licence* and €3,950/year for *Master*. However, numerous public universities grant institutional fee waivers reducing tuition to the domestic baseline rate: **€170/year for Licence** and **€243/year for Master**. Private *Grandes Écoles* range from €5,000 to €18,000+/year.
- **Undergraduate Entry (*Licence*):**  
  Pakistani HSSC is recognized for *Licence 1* (L1) consideration (recommended 70%+ score). 2-year Bachelor's or university credits can apply for Advanced Standing into *Licence 2* or *3*.
- **Graduate Entry (M1 vs. M2):**  
  Pakistani 4-year HEC Bachelor's qualifies for Master 1 (standard 2-year track) or direct Master 2 (specialized 1-year track for candidates with strong relevant background / CGPA ≥ 3.0).

#### B. The *Études en France* (EeF) & Campus France Mechanism
- **Mandatory Centralized Procedure:** Pakistani residents must apply through Campus France Pakistan via the *Études en France* (EeF) portal.
- **Application Window:** Typically opens **October 1** and closes **November 18** (for the subsequent September intake).
- **Application Capacity:** Up to 7 public university programs per single campaign dossier (processing fee: PKR 30,000).
- **Campus France Interview:** Mandatory pre-consular academic interview at Campus France centers in Islamabad, Lahore, or Karachi.

#### C. Pakistani Document Attestation & Legalization Pipeline
- BISE -> IBCC -> MOFA for secondary school credentials.
- HEC -> MOFA for university degrees and transcripts.
- Authorized French translation (performed or certified by *Alliance Française* in Pakistan).

#### D. Financial Solvency & Living Expenses
- Mandatory living expense threshold: minimum statutory benchmark of **€615 per month** (€7,380 per academic year).
- Proof formats: Personal blocked/liquid bank statement (with 6-month transaction history) or notarized French/Pakistani financial guarantor affidavit (*Prise en Charge*).

#### E. Consular Visa Execution (VLS-TS)
- Filing via France-Visas portal -> in-person biometrics and document dossier submission at VFS Global in Islamabad, Lahore, or Karachi -> issuance of Long-Stay Student Visa with Resident Permit status (*Visa de Long Séjour valant Titre de Séjour* / VLS-TS).
- Post-arrival validation with the French Office of Immigration and Integration (OFII) and registration with the national student health care system and *CAF* housing allowance.

### 2.3 Italy

#### A. Institutional Framework & Entry Routes
- **Institutional Types:** State Universities (*Università Statali*) and Non-state/Private Universities. Public university tuition is income-tiered (*ISEE-Parificato*), typically ranging from €156/year (statutory minimum regional tax) to €2,500/year max.
- **Undergraduate Entry (*Laurea Triennale*):**  
  Requires **12 years of primary/secondary schooling + 1 additional year** of university education (or Cambridge A-Levels / 1-year Foundation Course). A standard 12-year Pakistani HSSC alone requires bridging.
- **Graduate Entry (*Laurea Magistrale*):**  
  Requires an HEC-recognized 4-year Bachelor's degree (16 years of education). University departments evaluate syllabus module alignment and minimum GPA thresholds.
- **Entrance Tests & Language:**
  - Bachelor's programs frequently require TOLC (CISIA Online Tests) or TIL (Politecnico di Torino). IMAT required for Medicine.
  - English proficiency: IELTS Academic (minimum 6.0/6.5) or certified university English Medium of Instruction (MOI) if explicitly accepted by the specific department.

#### B. Centralized Pre-Enrollment & Credential Validation
- **Universitaly Portal (*universitaly.it*):** Centralized national platform where students register their target program, upload acceptance letters, and submit their pre-enrollment request for university and embassy validation.
- **Credential Equivalence:**  
  Italian authorities require either:
  1. **Declaration of Value (*Dichiarazione di Valore* / DOV):** Issued by the Italian diplomatic mission in Pakistan (Embassy Islamabad or Consulate Karachi).
  2. **CIMEA Certificates:** Official online Statements of Comparability and Verification issued by the Italian credential evaluation body.

#### C. Pakistani Document Attestation & Legalization Pipeline
- Primary verification by BISE/IBCC/HEC -> MOFA Apostille/attestation.
- Authorized translation into Italian by embassy-registered sworn translators.
- All documents (degrees, transcripts, FRC, sponsorships) must be formatted and presented with translation apostilles.

#### D. Regional Scholarships (DSU / EDISU / ER.GO / ALiSEO)
- Need-based regional scholarships awarded based on the student's family economic condition (*ISEE-Parificato* calculation).
- Benefits: Full tuition waiver, free or subsidized student accommodation, subsidized campus meals, and an annual cash stipend (ranging from €4,000 to €8,000+ depending on resident/non-resident status).

#### E. Consular Visa Execution (National Visa Type D - Studio)
- Submission via Gerry's / VFS Global to the Italian Embassy in Islamabad or Italian Consulate General in Karachi.
- Mandatory dossier items: Signed Universitaly Pre-Enrollment Summary, DOV or CIMEA receipt, valid passport, 6-month sponsor bank statements, sponsor FBR tax returns (last 3 consecutive years), family registration certificate (FRC), Schengen travel insurance (€30,000 minimum cover), and accommodation proof.
- Post-arrival: Request *Codice Fiscale* and apply for *Permesso di Soggiorno* within 8 working days.

### 2.4 Romania

#### A. Institutional Framework & Entry Routes
- **Institutional Types:** State Accredited Universities and Private Universities (regulated by ARACIS). Full European Union member, integrated into the Schengen area for air and sea borders.
- **Tuition Range:** Non-EU international tuition typically ranges from **€2,000 to €5,000 per academic year** (higher for Medicine/Dentistry: €7,000–€8,500/year).
- **Direct Undergraduate Eligibility:**  
  Unlike Germany and Italy, Romanian universities **directly accept the Pakistani 12-year HSSC (Intermediate / FSC / ICS)** for 3-year or 4-year Bachelor's degree admissions without requiring a foundation year.
- **Language Requirements:**
  - Direct entry to English-taught programs requires IELTS Academic (5.5–6.0 minimum) or verified English MOI.
  - Students without English proficiency or pursuing Romanian-taught faculties enroll in the 1-year **Romanian Preparatory Language Year (*An Pregătitor*)**.

#### B. Dual-Stage Statutory Admission Mechanism
Admission requires a mandatory two-tier validation:
1. **Stage 1 (Institutional Review):** University evaluates academic dossier and issues a conditional institutional acceptance letter.
2. **Stage 2 (Ministry Authorization):** The university submits the dossier to the **Romanian Ministry of Education (*Ministerul Educației*)** for national degree equivalence verification.
3. **Outcome:** Issuance of the official **Letter of Acceptance to Studies (*Scrisoare de Acceptare la Studii*)**. This document is legally required to apply for the Romanian student visa.

#### C. Post-Admission Protocol & Advance Tuition Payment
- **Mandatory Wire Transfer:** Upon receipt of the Ministry Acceptance Letter, the applicant must remit the full first-year tuition fee via international bank wire (SWIFT) or verified transfer platform directly to the university account.
- **Tuition Payment Receipt:** University issues an official fee payment receipt, which is a strictly mandatory document in the consular visa file.

#### D. Pakistani Document Attestation Pipeline
- Secondary credentials: BISE -> IBCC -> MOFA Apostille/attestation.
- Higher credentials: HEC online verification & stamp -> MOFA Apostille/attestation.
- Authorized Romanian translation and notary public certification.

#### E. Consular Visa Execution (Long-Stay Student Visa D/SD)
- Filing through the official Romanian electronic visa portal: **`eviza.mae.ro`**.
- Submission of physical file and biometric interview at the **Embassy of Romania in Islamabad**.
- Financial solvency benchmark: Bank statement demonstrating approximately **€8,000 to €10,300** maintained in a Pakistani bank account (calculated based on Romanian gross national minimum wage for the duration of the permit).
- Post-arrival: In-person university matriculation and application to the General Inspectorate for Immigration (*Inspectoratul General pentru Imigrări* / IGI) for a temporary residence permit card (*Permis de Ședere*).
